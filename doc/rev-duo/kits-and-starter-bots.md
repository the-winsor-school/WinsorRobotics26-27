# REV DUO Kits & Official Starter Bots Index

> **Winsor Robotics FTC Resource Directory**  
> Component: Assembly Manuals, Kit Guides, and Competition Reference Bots

REV Robotics publishes official step-by-step assembly guides for their commercial kits and releases complete "Starter Bot" reference designs every season at FTC Kickoff. These resources provide proven mechanical patterns that students can analyze, build, and adapt.

## 5. How Winsor Students Should Use These Designs

When starting a new robot:
1. **Never copy blindly:** A starter bot is built to be simple and accessible, not optimized for world-championship performance. Treat it as a mechanical baseline.
2. **Decompose into mechanisms:** Study how REV engineers mounted the motor, how they tensioned the belt, or how they supported the cantilevered shaft.
3. **Map to our Software Architecture:** When you decide on a mechanism from a starter bot, write its dedicated `MechComponent` class following our [NewRobotDesignWorkflow.md](../NewRobotDesignWorkflow.md)!
